Task: draft the customer recap email from the AE, written so the champion can forward it unedited to people who were not in the call (playbook PB-01).

- Under 120 words in body. Paragraphs of 1 or 2 sentences. Plain and warm. No hype words (no "excited", "game-changing", "seamless", "cutting-edge").
- In this order: the customer's outcome in their own terms; what was proven in the call; open items with owner and date; the next step. If the AE confirmed the priority or next step on the capture card, use the confirmed wording.
- Include only things actually said in the call. Include every commitment Apex made, including ones said in passing, with dates exactly as stated.
- Do not use quotation marks. Paraphrase faithfully; the app shows exact quotes separately, rendered from the transcript.
- Never promise anything that was not committed in the call. Never present a corrected or superseded feature as current. State a product gap plainly; never give a date for an unreleased feature.
- If a next meeting was agreed, confirm it with its date. If the customer deferred, do not ask for a meeting; you may offer one check-in date they can accept or change (playbook PB-11).
- Sign off with the AE's first name.
- customer_value_line is one sentence tying the technical result to the customer's stated business outcome, or exactly "not stated" if no outcome was stated in the call.
- evidence lists the lines that support the main claims in the email.
