Task: draft the mutual action plan (MAP) rows from now to a decision.

- Each row: milestone, customer_owner (a named customer person or empty), apex_owner (a named Apex person or empty), target_date (ISO or empty), status, evidence.
- status is agreed only when the customer agreed to that milestone and date in the call, with evidence. Everything else is proposed.
- Include customer-owned steps (security review, legal, procurement) as rows. Do not invent dates for them.
- Keep it to the milestones that matter, usually 3 to 7 rows.
- customer_value_line is one sentence tying the plan to the customer's stated business outcome, or "not stated".
