Task: assess the deal state and recommend at most 3 next steps.

deal_state is one of: ready_to_advance, open_technical_gap, missing_stakeholder, competitive_risk, waiting_on_customer, low_information. deal_state_why is 25 words or fewer.

Next steps:
- Rank them. The first is the single most important thing to do now.
- Include a step only if a typical AE would probably not do it unprompted. Sending a generic thank you or "following up" does not qualify. Fewer than 3 is normal. Never pad.
- Each step cites one playbook_rule_id (PB-01 to PB-10) and at least one evidence item from the transcript.
- why is 25 words or fewer and says why a typical AE might miss this step.
- owner is a named person (AE or SC by name, or the customer contact for customer actions).
- due_by is an ISO date on a business day after the as-of date, or empty when no date makes sense.
- action_type is one of: send_recap, book_meeting, send_material, route_to_sc, reach_stakeholder, support_customer_process, flag_deal_desk, resolve_with_champion, other.
- release_card_id is the card the SC should use, or empty.
- The action is one sentence a rep can act on in under 10 minutes. Refer to what this customer said, not generic sales advice.

Special cases:
- When progress waits on a customer-owned process, set deal_state to waiting_on_customer if no date is known. Steps must help that process (send what it needs, sized to its deadline). Never recommend checking in, chasing, or pushing for a meeting the customer deferred.
- When two customer people contradict each other on a requirement, flag it and recommend resolving it with the champion. Do not pick a side.
- When very little was said, use low_information and recommend at most 2 small steps. Do not infer intent from silence.

watch_items: add one for each competitor mentioned (name the competitor and the release card to use, if any), and for any other risk the AE should keep an eye on. Leave empty if none.

The MEDDPICC gaps listed in the context are fields still unknown after the demo. Use them only if closing one is among the most important steps.
