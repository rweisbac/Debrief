Task: extract a structured record of this TV demo.

Use the transcript first. Use the CRM context and the close-the-demo checklist only where noted.

Fill every field. Use empty lists when nothing applies.
- business_outcome: the outcome the customer said they want, in their terms. stated is false if no customer said it in this call; CRM discovery notes alone do not count.
- agreed_next_step: exists is true only if a customer agreed to a specific next meeting or action with a date in this call. A rep proposing something, or "we will be in touch", is not agreement. date is ISO (YYYY-MM-DD) or empty.
- objections: concerns or pushback from the customer, including politely worded ones. Say what the concern is really about when later lines reveal it. status is resolved, partially_resolved, or open. answer_given is what Apex said, or empty.
- open_technical_items: technical questions not fully answered in the call, with the owner who must close them.
- integration_patterns: how the customer agreed the systems would connect.
- commitments: anything anyone said they would do, including things said in passing. side is apex or customer. by_when is ISO or empty when no day was given. Resolve weekday names using the demo date given in the context.
- stakeholders: everyone present, plus anyone named or described who matters to the decision but was absent. why_they_matter is one short phrase.
- competitor_mentions: named competitors only.
- buying_signals and risk_signals: strength is strong, moderate, or weak. qualifier records what limits the signal (who said it, what followed it), or empty.
- exact_numbers: value copied exactly as spoken, with what it measures.
- customer_dependencies: customer-owned processes that must happen first (security review, legal, procurement, internal alignment). due is ISO or empty. blocks_progress is true when Apex cannot move the deal forward until it is done.
- contradictions: places where two customer people disagree on something that matters.
- corrections: statements later corrected in the call or by a release card. Record the original and the corrected version.
- checklist_mismatches: compare each close-the-demo checklist field with the transcript. Report a field when it is blank but the transcript has the information, or when it states something the transcript does not support.

Every list item needs at least one evidence item.
