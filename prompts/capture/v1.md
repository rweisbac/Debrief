Task: pre-fill 4 judgment fields for the AE after this TV demo. The AE will confirm or correct each one in under a minute, so be exact and say how sure you are.

Rules for every field:
- Never invent facts. Use the transcript first. Use the CRM context only to identify people and roles.
- basis is "stated" when a customer said it in this call, "inferred" when you reasoned it from the call or CRM without anyone saying it, and "none" when there is no basis. With basis "none", leave the value empty (or the least committal enum value) and set model_confidence to 0.0.
- model_confidence is your probability, from 0.0 to 1.0, that the AE would accept the value unchanged. Lower it when the transcript is ambiguous, when people disagree, or when a later line weakens an earlier one.
- evidence lists the lines the value rests on. Each excerpt is copied word for word from that line, under 20 words. Inferred values still cite the lines they were inferred from.

Fields:
- priority_why_now: the buyer's real priority and the trigger for acting now, in the customer's terms, 25 words or fewer. A priority with no stated trigger is still a priority; say "no trigger stated" in the value rather than inventing one.
- champion_status: "strong" when a customer person in the call is actively moving the deal internally (owns a next step, books the economic buyer, offers to carry material to absent people); "unclear" when someone is positive but has not acted, or says the decision is not theirs; "absent" when nobody on the customer side is doing this. champion_name is that person, or empty. economic_buyer_identified is "yes" when someone named who signs or owns the budget, "no" when nobody did, "unclear" when it was hinted but not confirmed. economic_buyer_name is that person, or empty.
- top_risk: the single risk most likely to stall this deal in the next 30 days. One of budget, timeline, competitor, security_review, integration, no_urgency, other. note is one line saying what the risk is in this deal (required for other).
- next_step: the next step the customer agreed to in this call. A rep proposing something, or "we will be in touch", is not agreement; then leave value and date empty with basis "none". date is the ISO date said in the call, resolved from the demo date given in the context, or empty.
