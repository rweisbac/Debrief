Task: grade one recommended next step for an AE after a technical validation demo, against the best practice rubric below. Answer each criterion pass or fail on its own. Do not average. Be strict: when unsure, fail the criterion.

The rubric comes from published research on buying groups and sales follow-up (summarized in the playbook). It rewards steps that help the customer's buying group decide without the seller in the room.

- r1_grounded: passes when the step names a fact, person, outcome or open item from this call. Fails when it could apply to any deal. Pass: "Send the COO a time-to-value plan that leads with catching late loads while dispatch can still reroute." Fail: "Send a value-focused follow-up."
- r2_binding_constraint: passes when the step addresses what currently prevents the customer's next internal decision, as shown in the transcript. Fails when it improves something that is not blocking. Pass (security committee gates everything): "Deliver the full security packet, including the written SSO design, before the Oct 9 committee." Fail in the same deal: "Send a customer case study."
- r3_buyer_enabling: passes when the output helps a customer stakeholder complete a buying task and can be forwarded without the rep. Fails when it only serves the seller (internal flags, chasers). Pass: "A 1 page brief for the champion's boss comparing extending the incumbent against adopting Apex, on their workload." Fail: "Flag the competitor to deal desk" as the customer-facing step.
- r4_owned_dated: passes when the step names an owner (AE, SC or a named customer role) and a date or deadline. Fails without an owner or timing. Pass: "SC sends the row-level access answer on Tuesday Oct 6 and the AE confirms it in writing." Fail: "Look into the partner hierarchy question."
- r5_honest_bounded: passes when claims are limited to the customer's numbers, gaps are stated, and no roadmap dates or guessed timelines appear. Fails on a promised release date, a guessed go-live or unverified savings. Pass: "State that nested claims have no release date and lay out the token-change workaround with its 6 to 8 week review." Fail: "Tell them nested claims are coming next quarter."
- r6_respects_process: passes when the step supports the customer's stated process and pace, or offers rather than presses. Fails when it pushes a meeting the customer deferred or sends checking-in messages. Pass: "Offer a check-in date the champion can accept or change." Fail after "we'll be in touch": "Call Monday to lock in a meeting."
- r7_beyond_hygiene: passes when the step goes beyond baseline hygiene (recap sent, invite sent, CRM updated, internal flag raised). Fails when it is only a hygiene item. Pass: "Coach the champion on how her boss will challenge the deal and give her the answer." Fail: "Update the opportunity stage in CRM."
- r8_feasible: passes when the AE or SC can complete it within 1 to 10 business days with assets that exist or can be built. Substantial work such as a packet or a phased plan is fine. Fails when it depends on things outside the seller's control (a product release, a customer decision). Pass: "Draft a phased onboarding plan with hours by role." Fail: "Ship nested claims support before the committee."

best_practice_rule: the playbook rule ID (PB-01 to PB-16) this step best matches, or "none".

critique: one sentence naming the weakest criterion and what would fix it.
